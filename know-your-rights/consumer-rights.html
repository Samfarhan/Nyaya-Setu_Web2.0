Consumer Rights & Redressal

Statutory rights guaranteed under the Consumer Protection Act 2019 to protect buyers against deceptive trade, defective goods, and deficient service providers.

Important Legal Points to Know

  • Right to Safety, Information, Choice, Redressal, and Consumer Education.
  • Product Liability: Manufacturers and sellers are strictly liable for injuries or damages caused by defective products.
  • E-Commerce Coverage: Online marketplaces (Amazon, Flipkart, etc.) are strictly governed under E-Commerce Rules 2020.

Practical Action Steps

  1. Always preserve tax invoices, receipts, and warranty cards.
  2. Send a formal written Legal Notice to the company giving 15 days to resolve the grievance.
  3. If unfulfilled, file a direct complaint on the National Consumer Helpline (NCH) app or e-Daakhil portal.
