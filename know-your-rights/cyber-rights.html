Cyber Rights & Digital Privacy

Rights shielding citizens against online financial scams, identity theft, cyberstalking, and unauthorized data breaches under the IT Act 2000.

Important Legal Points to Know

  • Helpline 1930 connects directly to bank fraud monitoring nodes to freeze stolen amounts instantaneously.
  • DPDP Act 2023 grants citizens the right to data erasure and privacy consent withdraw.

Practical Action Steps

  1. Call 1930 immediately within 1 hour of unauthorized bank transactions.
  2. Take screenshots of transactional SMS, bank statements, and fraudster numbers.
  3. Lodge a complaint on www.cybercrime.gov.in.
