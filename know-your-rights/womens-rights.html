Women's Legal Rights & Protections

Special legal safeguards enshrined under the Constitution of India, BNS, and specialized acts protecting women against domestic violence, workplace harassment, and illegal arrest.

Important Legal Points to Know

  • Zero FIR Mandate: A woman victim of a sexual offence can register an FIR at ANY police station regardless of jurisdiction.
  • Sunset Protection: Under BNSS Section 43(4), women cannot be arrested after sunset and before sunrise except by a female officer with prior Magistrate sanction.
  • Right to Confidentiality: Section 72 of BNS prohibits publishing or revealing the identity of victims of sexual offences.
  • Workplace Protection: The POSH Act 2013 mandates an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) in every organization with 10+ employees.

Practical Action Steps

  1. For emergency help, dial 112 (National Emergency) or 1091 (Women Helpline).
  2. For workplace harassment, submit a written complaint to your company's Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) within 3 months.
  3. Keep digital backups of emails, WhatsApp messages, or call logs as electronic evidence.
