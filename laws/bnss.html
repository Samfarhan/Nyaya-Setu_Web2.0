Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023

Governs the procedural framework for police investigations, arrests, bail, trials, and court administration in criminal matters.

Key Concepts & Structural Highlights

  • Mandatory forensic investigation for offences punishable by 7+ years imprisonment.
  • Strict timelines: Charge sheets within 90 days; trial judgments within 45 days of conclusion.
  • Statutory recognition of Zero FIR, e-FIR, and digital/audio-video recording of searches and seizures.
  • Enhanced safeguards for arrests of elderly or infirm citizens.

Practical Relevance

Defines police powers, bail application procedure (Sec 478-496), FIR filing rules (Sec 173), and Magistrate jurisdictions.

Official Reference: Ministry of Home Affairs Guidelines
