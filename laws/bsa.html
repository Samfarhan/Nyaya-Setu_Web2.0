Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023

Modernizes the rules of evidence admissibility, giving equal legal standing to electronic records, digital signatures, and server logs.

Key Concepts & Structural Highlights

  • Electronic & Digital Records recognized as primary evidence.
  • Standardized admissibility for cloud storage, emails, WhatsApp logs, and smartphone recordings.
  • Protection of spousal communication and legal professional privilege.

Practical Relevance

Crucial for establishing guilt or innocence in court trials through physical and digital evidence.

Official Reference: Gazette of India 2023
