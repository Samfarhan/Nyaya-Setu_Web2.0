Simplified explanations for legal jargon, Latin maxims, BNS/IPC sections, and constitutional terms.

First Information Report (FIR)

Criminal Law

Initial written document prepared by police after receiving information about a cognizable offence.

BNSS Section 173 / CrPC Section 154 Crime Registration

Zero FIR

Criminal Law

An FIR registered at any police station regardless of geographical jurisdiction, later transferred to the appropriate station.

BNSS Mandate / Ministry of Home Affairs Guidelines Any Jurisdiction

Bail

Criminal Law

Judicial release of an accused person from police or prison custody pending trial upon executing a bond or security.

BNSS Section 478-496 / CrPC Section 436-439 Pre-Trial Liberty
Read Full Explanation

Anticipatory Bail

Criminal Law

A pre-arrest court order protecting a person from being detained by police if arrested for a non-bailable offence.

BNSS Section 482 / CrPC Section 438 Pre-Arrest Protection

Cognizable Offence

Criminal Law

A serious crime where police have authority to arrest without a court warrant and initiate investigation immediately.

BNSS Section 2(1)(g) / CrPC Section 2(c) Warrantless Arrest

Non-Cognizable Offence

Criminal Law

A minor or less severe crime where police cannot arrest without a warrant or investigate without court permission.

BNSS Section 2(1)(l) / CrPC Section 2(l) Warrant Required

Charge Sheet (Final Police Report)

A formal police report submitted to court detailing evidence, witnesses, and specific criminal charges against the accused.
