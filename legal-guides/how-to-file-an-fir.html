How to File an FIR in India (Step-by-Step Guide)

A practical walkthrough on registering a First Information Report (FIR) under Section 173 of BNSS 2023, knowing what details to include, and what to do if police refuse registration.

Step-by-Step Procedure

1

Identify the Cognizable Offence

Ensure the crime involves a cognizable offence (e.g. theft, assault, robbery, cyber fraud). If minor, police will log an NCR instead.

2

Visit the Police Station or Lodge Zero FIR

Go to the nearest police station having territorial jurisdiction. In emergency or out-of-station scenarios, request a Zero FIR.

3

Provide Oral or Written Statement

Narrate the facts clearly: Date, Time, Exact Location, Accused Name/Description, Witnesses, and Stolen Property details. The police officer will write it down.

4

Verify and Sign the FIR Copy

Read the written FIR statement carefully. Ensure no facts were omitted or distorted. Sign the statement.

5

Obtain Free Stamped Copy of FIR

Under BNSS Section 173(2), collect the official stamped copy of the FIR with the unique FIR Number immediately, completely free of charge.

Important Warning:

Never give false or misleading statements in an FIR. Filing a false FIR is punishable under Section 248 of BNS 2023.

Do not leave the station without your official free stamped copy of the FIR.
