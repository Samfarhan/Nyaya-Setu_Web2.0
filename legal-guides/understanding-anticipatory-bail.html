How Anticipatory Bail Works in India

Understanding pre-arrest protection under Section 482 BNSS, eligible offences, court criteria, and conditions imposed.

Step-by-Step Procedure

1

Evaluate Apprehension of Arrest

Consult a legal advocate if there is reasonable ground to believe a non-bailable FIR or false complaint is imminent.

2

File Application in Sessions Court or High Court

Draft an anticipatory bail application highlighting lack of flight risk, cooperation with investigation, and bogus allegations.

3

Interim Protection & Final Hearing

The judge may grant interim protection against arrest while calling for police status reports before passing final orders.

Important Warning:

Anticipatory bail does not mean you can evade police inquiry; you are mandated to join investigation whenever called.
