Rights in Daily Life

Situations where you need protection the most.

Police & Arrest Interaction

  • Grounds of Arrest: Police must inform you of the exact reason for arrest immediately.
  • No Detention > 24 Hrs: Cannot be held without being presented to a Magistrate.
  • Right to Inform: You have the right to call a friend or relative to inform them of your location.
  • Right to Lawyer: You have the right to consult your lawyer during interrogation.

Traffic & Roadside

  • Keys Snatching Illegal: Traffic police cannot forcibly take your vehicle keys.
  • Uniform Mandatory: Officer must be in uniform with a nameplate to issue a fine.
  • Demand Receipt: Always demand an official challan receipt on spot.
  • Digital Documents Valid: DigiLocker and mParivahan copies are legally valid under IT Act.

Women Safety & Protections

  • No Sunset Arrest: Women cannot be arrested after sunset and before sunrise except in extraordinary cases with Magistrate sanction.
  • Female Escort: Arrest must be carried out by or in presence of a female police officer.
  • Zero FIR: Can file an FIR at any police station irrespective of crime location.

Consumer & Retail Rights

  • No MRP Surcharges: Retailers cannot charge above Maximum Retail Price (MRP).
  • Right to Refuse Mandatory Tip: Service charge at restaurants is strictly voluntary.
  • Right to Refund: Defective products must be replaced or refunded under Consumer Protection Act 2019.

Action Plan in Real Scenarios

Click on any scenario to reveal step-by-step guidance.

Police Stop You for Late-Night Checking

  1. Remain calm and request the officer's identity badge or nameplate.
  2. Show required documents via DigiLocker or mParivahan.
  3. If detained, ask for the exact reason and record details politely.

Cyber Fraud or Online Money Theft

  1. Immediately call the national cyber helpline at 1930.
  2. Block affected bank accounts, cards, or digital wallets via mobile banking app.
  3. File a formal complaint online at cybercrime.gov.in within the golden hour.
