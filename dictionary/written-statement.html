Written Statement

The formal defense document filed by a defendant responding line-by-line to the allegations in a plaint.

Legal Meaning & Statutory Context

Governed by Order 8 of CPC, the written statement must be filed within 30 days (extendable to 90 days) from receipt of court summons, setting up defenses, counter-claims, or set-offs.

Detailed Plain-Language Explanation

Failure to file a written statement within the statutory timeframe can lead to the court passing an ex-parte judgment against the defendant.

Practical Example

The tenant files a written statement denying rent default and presenting digital bank transaction receipts showing timely payment.

Where Used:

Civil Courts

Statutory Reference:

CPC Order 8
