Non-Cognizable Offence

A minor or less severe crime where police cannot arrest without a warrant or investigate without court permission.

Legal Meaning & Statutory Context

Defined under Section 2(1)(l) of BNSS 2023, an offence for which a police officer has no authority to arrest without a warrant.

Detailed Plain-Language Explanation

Offences like simple hurt, defamation, public nuisance, or minor forgery are non-cognizable. If a victim reports such a crime, police record an NCR (Non-Cognizable Report) and direct the complainant to approach the Magistrate court.

Practical Example

A neighbor commits criminal defamation by publishing false rumors online. The victim approaches police, who record a Non-Cognizable Report (NCR) and inform the victim to file a complaint before the Magistrate.

Where Used:

Magistrate Courts, NCR Reporting

Statutory Reference:

BNSS Section 2(1)(l) / CrPC Section 2(l)
