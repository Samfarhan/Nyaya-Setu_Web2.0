Injunction

A court order restraining a party from carrying out a specific action or compelling them to restore a previous condition.

Legal Meaning & Statutory Context

Equitable remedy issued under the Specific Relief Act 1963 and Code of Civil Procedure (CPC Order 39) preventing irreparable injury or infringement of property rights.

Detailed Plain-Language Explanation

Injunctions can be temporary (interim stay orders issued during a pending lawsuit) or permanent (final decree granted at judgment). They protect property rights, trademarks, and prevent unlawful eviction.

Practical Example

A developer attempts to demolish a boundary wall on adjacent private land without consent. The property owner files a civil suit and obtains a temporary injunction halting demolition until ownership is determined.

Where Used:

Civil Courts, Commercial Courts

Statutory Reference:

CPC Order 39 / Specific Relief Act 1963
