Phishing & Identity Theft

Cybercrime involving stolen personal credentials, passwords, OTPs, or financial data via deceptive communications.

Legal Meaning & Statutory Context

Punishable under Sections 66C (Identity Theft) and 66D (Cheating by Personation using Computer Resource) of the Information Technology Act 2000 and BNS 2023.

Detailed Plain-Language Explanation

Phishing scams trick victims into revealing sensitive banking information via fake emails, SMS (smishing), or spoofed websites. Indian law mandates criminal penalties, asset freeze, and restitution through Cyber Crime Cells.

Practical Example

A user receives a fraudulent SMS posing as an official bank alert to update KYC. The user enters their net-banking password on a cloned link. The fraudster steals ₹1 Lakh. The user files a complaint under IT Act Section 66D.

Where Used:

Cyber Crime Police Cells, IT Adjudicating Officers

Statutory Reference:

IT Act 2000 Sec 66C/66D & BNS Sec 318
