Zero FIR

An FIR registered at any police station regardless of geographical jurisdiction, later transferred to the appropriate station.

Legal Meaning & Statutory Context

Introduced to eliminate jurisdictional delays in emergency criminal cases, a Zero FIR is registered without a station number (numbered '0') and transferred to the competent jurisdiction.

Detailed Plain-Language Explanation

When a crime occurs in one location but is reported in another, police cannot refuse registration on grounds of lack of territorial jurisdiction. They must record the Zero FIR, initiate immediate emergency action, and transfer the case files.

Practical Example

A passenger is assaulted on a train traveling between Delhi and Jaipur. Upon arriving in Jaipur, she lodges a Zero FIR at Jaipur Railway Police Station. Jaipur police register it immediately and transfer it to Delhi police for investigation.

Where Used:

Emergency Crime Reporting, Sexual Offences, Accidents, Interstate Crimes

Statutory Reference:

BNSS Mandate / Ministry of Home Affairs Guidelines
