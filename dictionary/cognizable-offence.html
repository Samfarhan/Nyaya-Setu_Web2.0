Cognizable Offence

A serious crime where police have authority to arrest without a court warrant and initiate investigation immediately.

Legal Meaning & Statutory Context

Defined under Section 2(1)(g) of BNSS 2023, an offence in which a police officer may, in accordance with the First Schedule or under any other law, arrest without warrant.

Detailed Plain-Language Explanation

Cognizable offences include severe crimes such as murder, kidnapping, rape, dacoity, and major cyber frauds. Police have statutory powers to investigate without prior judicial authorization.

Practical Example

Police receive information about a robbery in progress. They rush to the spot and arrest the suspect immediately without waiting to obtain a warrant from a Magistrate.

Where Used:

Criminal Code, Police Investigations

Statutory Reference:

BNSS Section 2(1)(g) / CrPC Section 2(c)
